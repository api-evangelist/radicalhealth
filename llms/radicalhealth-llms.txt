# Radical Health

> AI-powered expert reports for cancer patients - synthesizing oncology research, clinical trials, and patient outcomes for individual patients in days.

## What we do

- Personalized cancer treatment reports generated from a patient’s medical records
- 24/7 AI nurse navigator (Alice) personalized to each patient’s case
- Clinical trial matching against 48,800+ active US trials
- Synthesis of 5.3 million research papers and 11.8 million patient journeys

## Who we serve

Cancer patients (and their care partners) in the United States who want clarity on treatment options beyond what fits in a 30-minute oncologist appointment.

## Trust signals

- Guided by oncologists from UCLA, Johns Hopkins, Stanford, and Kaiser Permanente
- HIPAA, SOC 2 Type II certified
- Backed by Khosla Ventures
- Reports for informational and educational purposes only - not medical advice
